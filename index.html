About me

I am an HCI researcher with a background in Data Science, interested in understanding how people interact with technology and how data can be used to uncover patterns in user behavior. My work sits at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, UX research, and data-driven approaches to understanding users.
I am particularly interested in using behavioral data to identify patterns in users’ experiences, understand the factors that shape their decisions and interactions, and translate these insights into better-designed products and services.
